Courses Offered

Computer Science & Engineering

The Computer Science and Engineering (CSE) department at ABES Engineering College in Ghaziabad offers a four-year, NBA-accredited B.Tech program affiliated with AKTU. The curriculum covers core programming, algorithms, and specialized ABES Engineering College Courses like AI-ML and Data Science, paired with structured industry training.

Electronics & Communication Engineering

The Electronics and Communication Engineering (ECE) program at ABES Engineering College in Ghaziabad is a 4-year, NBA-accredited B.Tech course with an intake capacity of 180 students. Affiliated with Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU), the curriculum covers circuit design, VLSI, and embedded systems.

Electrical and Computer Engineering

The 4-year undergraduate B.Tech in Electrical and Computer Engineering at ABES Engineering College in Ghaziabad has an intake capacity of 120 students. Affiliated with Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU) and approved by AICTE, the program integrates core electrical concepts with modern computing and automation technologies.

Mechanical Engineering

The Mechanical Engineering course at ABES Engineering College in Ghaziabad is a 4-year, full-time B.Tech program affiliated with Dr. APJ Abdul Kalam Technical University (AKTU). It requires a 10+2 with 45% marks in Physics and Mathematics, plus valid JEE Main scores
